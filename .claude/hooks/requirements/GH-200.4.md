### GH-200.4
- text: The split round-trips: every `GH-` entry `requirements.md` held before it
  is a file under `requirements/`, byte for byte the block it was, and the suite
  holds a checksum and a length of each, taken off `requirements.md` at 4e91496
  rather than off the files.
- from: #200
- kind: defect-permitting
- status: active
- direction: static: it reads the files against a literal
- note: THE TRADE, taken knowingly. The literal holds those 117 entries still, so
  an edit to one of them -- a gap marker taken off, a note amended -- goes red
  until its token moves with it. That is #200's second design point, that a
  ledger entry is immutable once written and a correction is a new entry that
  supersedes it, applied to the entries the split moved and to none written
  after them. It is also what "checked by the suite" cannot avoid: the suite
  runs in a shallow checkout with no history, so a comparison with the entries
  as they were has to carry them, and anything that carries them holds them
  still.
