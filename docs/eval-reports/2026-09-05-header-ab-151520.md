# The header A/B — 300 paired calls, and a registered rule that contradicted itself

| | |
|---|---|
| run | 2026-09-05, 15:15–15:28 +03 |
| branch | `dev-05` |
| commit at run | `3c936c3f9418` |
| probe | `scripts/probe_header_ab.py` |
| registered in | the *Registration* section of [`2026-09-05-provider-acceptance-pre-registration.md`](2026-09-05-provider-acceptance-pre-registration.md), written before the first call at the same commit |
| ticket | [#22](https://github.com/bgunyel/clause-and-effect/issues/22), under map [#6](https://github.com/bgunyel/clause-and-effect/issues/6) |
| subject | whether `X-OpenRouter-Metadata: enabled` distorts the request it rides on |
| spend | $0.009758 over 600 usable calls |

**Headline.** The registration's central premise — that the baseline arm would be
degenerate — **is false**, and its two registered categorical readings then
returned **opposite verdicts on the same data**. The categorical endpoint is
therefore recorded as **inconclusive by defect**, with no claim in either
direction, and a controlled re-run is owed. The three secondary endpoints are
unaffected by that defect and returned cleanly.

---

## 1. What ran

300 pairs, 600 calls, one process, sequential, leading arm alternating between
pairs. Request shape held fixed and identical to the judge's own:
`build_structured_llm` on DeepSeek V4 Flash under `function_calling`, `model_args`
straight from `llm_config`, so `provider: {'require_parameters': True}` and a
bound tool both reached the wire — the 23-endpoint pool, not the 29.

The precondition held on every pair:

| check | result |
|---|---|
| request bodies hash-equal within pair | 300/300 |
| header present on the header arm | 300/300 |
| header absent on the baseline arm | 300/300 |

So the two arms differed **only** in one request header, byte for byte, on every
pair. Every pair was usable: no timeout, no non-200, no retried call, nothing
dropped.

A wiring smoke run of 2 pairs preceded this, as registered. Its observations are
discarded and are not pooled here.

## 2. The primary endpoint, and why it returns nothing

| | header arm | baseline arm |
|---|---|---|
| Baidu | 299 | 299 |
| Relace | 1 | 1 |

Discordant pairs: **2 of 300**, at pairs 173 and 251. Clopper–Pearson 95%
one-sided upper bound at 2/300: **2.08%**.

**The marginal distributions are identical**, and the two discordances run in
**opposite directions** — one pair where the baseline arm was served by Relace and
the header arm by Baidu, one pair the other way round. (Deductively, not by
assumption: each arm holds exactly one non-Baidu observation, so the two must sit
on the two discordant pairs, one per arm; the baseline arm's `cached_tokens: 0`
lands on pair 173, which fixes 173 as the baseline's Relace and 251 as the
header's.) One discordance each way is the signature of a router that is not
deterministic, not of a treatment that steers it.

### The registration contradicts itself here

Two rows of the registered outcome table both apply to this data and they
disagree:

- **`≥1 flip` → *"The header distorts routing. #14's decision is reversed."*** By
  the letter, this fires: there were 2.
- **`≥2 providers observed in both arms` → a share comparison at the achieved n.**
  Also satisfied, and the share comparison is 299/1 against 299/1 — identical
  marginals, exact McNemar on 1 versus 1 discordant pairs giving p = 1.00.

The defect is in the registration, and it is named rather than softened: **the
assistant registered a categorical statistic with no control for baseline
non-determinism.** The flip count silently assumes the baseline arm returns the
same provider every time, so that any discordance must be the header's doing. That
assumption came from #13's degenerate n=3 and from §A.5's explicit expectation
that a degenerate baseline was *likely*. It is false: this pool routes away from
Baidu roughly 1 call in 300, and a paired design with no baseline-versus-baseline
arm cannot tell that noise from an effect.

**A pre-registration that contradicts itself cannot be repaired by picking the
branch one prefers.** Choosing *distortion* here would read a p = 1.00 discordance
as a positive result; choosing *no distortion* would be the friendlier restatement
that the Amendments rule exists to forbid. The refusal is symmetric, which is what
makes it a rule rather than a convenience:

> **Registered outcome, recorded as taken: inconclusive by defect on the
> categorical endpoint.** No claim is made in either direction. The design may not
> assert categorical non-distortion, and #14 is **not** reversed on this evidence.

What it costs to leave open is small, and is stated so nobody re-litigates it from
memory: #14's decision was registered reversible, the design's non-distortion
claim was already written conditionally, and it simply stays conditional.

## 3. The secondary endpoints — clean, and unaffected by that defect

These are powered by n regardless of how routing landed, which is why the
registration carried them.

**Wire latency.** Measured at the socket, around `httpx`'s `send`.

| | |
|---|---|
| header arm mean | 1.146 s |
| baseline arm mean | 1.151 s |
| paired difference (header − baseline) | **−4.2 ms** |
| 95% CI | **[−47.8, +39.3] ms** |
| registered margin, ±10% of baseline mean | ±115.1 ms |

The interval is contained in the registered margin. **The design may assert: no
latency penalty, with the paired difference bounded inside ±48 ms at n = 300.**
This is the first result in this effort that converts #13's *"no penalty
observed"* at n=3 into an interval, and it is the claim the run was worth running
for.

**Prompt-side cost.** Registered as exact equality, with any difference reported
individually and never averaged. It differed on **2 pairs of 300**:

| pair | header | baseline |
|---|---|---|
| 173 | 1.3804476e-05 | 3.1265e-05 |
| 251 | 1.8721e-05 | 1.3804476e-05 |

Those are pairs 173 and 251 — **exactly and only the two provider-discordant
pairs**. On the other 298 the prompt-side cost is identical to all digits at
`1.3804476e-05`. The registered finding fired; its cause is confounded with the
provider difference and is reported as confounded rather than resolved.

**`cached_tokens`.** Header arm: 256 on all 300. Baseline arm: 256 on 299 and 0 on
one — pair 173, again a provider-discordant pair. No pair differs where both arms
were served by the same provider.

**The pattern across all three secondaries is that every observed difference sits
on a provider-discordant pair and nowhere else.** That is a strong within-run
suggestion that the header changes nothing on the wire; it is recorded as a
suggestion, because it is a post-hoc reading of pairs selected by their outcome
and no rule registered it in advance.

## 4. The vocabulary check, and what it now covers

Registered assertion, on every header-arm response: top-level `provider`,
`openrouter_metadata.endpoints.available[selected].provider` and the `selected=`
clause of `openrouter_metadata.summary` spell the same endpoint.

**300 of 300 responses carried all three spellings, and all three agreed on every
one.** No disagreement, so nothing blocks the first production row on this ground.

**It now spans two providers rather than one** — Baidu and Relace — which is the
multi-provider observation #14 deferred to. The coverage is honest about its
shape: Baidu at n = 299, **Relace at n = 1**. The check is no longer bounded at a
single provider, and it is not yet broad; a third provider has never been seen at
all.

## 5. Facts recorded in passing

- **The router's own skew, measured for the first time.** On this model, this
  request shape, this day: **598 of 600 calls to Baidu, 2 to Relace (0.33%)**, out
  of a 23-endpoint pool. Organic routing concentrates almost completely.
- **`openrouter_metadata.attempt` was `1` on all 300 header-arm responses.** No
  fallback was exercised, so the multi-attempt shape of `openrouter_metadata` and
  of `provider_responses` **remains unobserved** — the same gap #13 recorded at
  n=3, now surviving n=300. It is a gap, not an absence.
- **The probe kept no raw bodies.** Everything above is derived from fields the
  probe chose to extract, which is why §2's assignment of Relace to arms had to be
  reconstructed deductively. A re-run should persist the raw response bodies of at
  least the discordant pairs.

## 6. What the design may say

Written out so the build does not have to re-derive it from this file:

- **Permitted:** *"Sending `X-OpenRouter-Metadata: enabled` carries no measurable
  latency penalty: over 300 paired calls on a fixed request shape the mean paired
  difference was −4.2 ms with a 95% CI of [−47.8, +39.3] ms, inside a
  pre-registered ±10% margin."*
- **Permitted:** *"The three spellings of the served provider agreed on 300 of 300
  header-carrying responses, across two providers."*
- **Not permitted:** any claim that the header does or does not affect routing.
  The categorical endpoint is inconclusive by defect and stays conditional.
- **Not permitted:** reading the prompt-side cost equality on 298 of 300 pairs as
  a cost claim. It was not registered that way, and the two exceptions are
  confounded with the provider difference.
